## What I Learned

Today I learned that machine learning models learn patterns from data instead of being explicitly programmed for every situation. I explored two common supervised learning algorithms: Logistic Regression and Decision Trees.

Logistic Regression is used for classification tasks, such as predicting whether an email is spam or not spam. Decision Trees make predictions by asking a series of questions and following branches based on the answers.

I also learned the basic model training workflow:

1. Collect data
2. Clean and prepare the data
3. Split the data into training and testing sets
4. Train the model
5. Evaluate its performance
6. Improve the model if necessary

## Key Takeaways

- Data quality greatly affects model performance.
- Different algorithms are suited to different problems.
- Model evaluation is important to ensure reliable predictions.

## Reflection

Understanding how models learn from data helped me appreciate how AI systems make decisions. I am beginning to see how these concepts can be applied to real-world applications such as recommendation systems and prediction tools.
