# Day 12 – Flutter Layout Widgets

**Date:** July 27, 2026

## Introduction

In Flutter, **layout widgets** are widgets that control how other widgets are arranged, positioned, and displayed on the screen. They help developers create responsive, organized, and attractive user interfaces (UI).

Layout widgets determine:

- The position of widgets.
- The size of widgets.
- The spacing between widgets.
- The alignment of widgets.

Without layout widgets, Flutter would not know how to display widgets properly on the screen.

---

# What are Layout Widgets?

A **layout widget** is a widget that arranges one or more child widgets within the available space.

Examples include:

- Row
- Column
- Container
- Stack
- Center
- Expanded
- Padding
- Align
- SizedBox
