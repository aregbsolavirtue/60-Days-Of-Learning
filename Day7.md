# Day 007 – Introduction to Natural Language Processing (NLP)

**Date:** July 20, 2026

## What I Learned

Today, I was introduced to **Natural Language Processing (NLP)**.

Natural Language Processing is a field of Artificial Intelligence (AI) that allows computers to understand, process, and work with human language.

Simply put, NLP helps computers understand the way people speak and write.

## Examples of NLP

Some everyday examples of NLP are:

- ChatGPT
- Google Translate
- Siri and Google Assistant
- Email spam filters
- Autocorrect on smartphones

## Why NLP is Important

Without NLP, computers would only understand numbers and programming languages. NLP makes it possible for computers to understand text and speech, making communication between humans and computers much easier.

## Key Takeaways

- NLP stands for **Natural Language Processing**.
- It is a branch of Artificial Intelligence.
- NLP helps computers understand human language.
- Many applications we use every day rely on NLP.
